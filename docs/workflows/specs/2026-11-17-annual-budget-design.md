# Annual budget control

Approved scope: implement the complete annual budget workflow requested by the user. Budget remains the canonical owner; PostgreSQL and existing permission/workflow providers remain in place.

Annual settings belong to a fiscal year. Stable catalog identities retain their existing foreign keys; year overlays own names, active state and the three routing sets. Existing immutable JSON, plan identities and financial history stay unchanged. All existing fiscal years remain open with no closure timestamp. The old inactive flag only blocked new project selection and did not prove closure; managers explicitly close years under the new obligation guards.

Authorized carryover plus annual funding defines each source envelope. A separately entered reserve and unit allocations must fit that envelope; the remainder is unallocated. Actual received funding is independently nullable. Existing years start unconfigured: historical project totals never become invented authorization or actual receipts. Managers confirm annual settings through the existing Manage permission and optimistic revision checks, while Finance records actual receipts through the existing payment permission. No fourth approval workflow is introduced.

Every operational write locks its fiscal years in UUID order before sorted projects, accounts and workflows. Final plan approval and adjustment posting check current planned plus adjusted commitments against school, source and unit ceilings in the same transaction. Amendments replace their previous plan contribution; transfers debit and credit before the final check. Closed years reject writes. Closing refuses submitted processes, reserved commitments or outstanding loans. Fiscal dates lock once any project has used the year. New activity dates must lie inclusively inside both project and year dates; legacy unknown dates remain null until explicitly supplied on a new save/submission.

The user explicitly authorized deleting all history for never-approved projects, including submitted, returned and rejected drafts. This overrides the former blanket preservation rule only for those projects. Accepted plans, amendment drafts attached to accepted projects and financial records remain protected. Narrow provider-owned deletion removes all exact Budget/Workflow/Notification descendants under sealed row authorization, never through a runtime GUC or globally disabled trigger. Empty/draft-only year deletion cannot erase retained projects or their historical year references.

The runtime switches to the canonical year APIs in the same change. Old binaries are prohibited after migrations 162/163; recovery is roll-forward. Applied migrations are immutable. Populated migration fixtures reconcile preserved JSON, identities and references and verify rollback/refusal. Focused PostgreSQL concurrency, lifecycle, cap and deletion tests plus generated API/consumer checks establish acceptance.
