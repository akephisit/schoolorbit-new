# Selected Delivery revision room scope

## Approved scope

Remove unrelated rooms/groups from the selected Delivery revision, for example M4/2 and M4/3 while retaining M6/1. Preserve the offering, retained group identities, names, settings, teachers, rosters, assessment configuration, scores and results. Whole-offering removal still removes the entire offering from that revision. This feature does not reset grading, create replacement offering instances, globally delete protected resources, or silently edit another draft/published snapshot.

The current failure is semantic: curriculum preview/apply look up registry offerings and groups across the term; Retain adds targets and inclusion recaptures registry associations. A removed group does not remove its offering target. Clearing a whole-offering exclusion consequently restores obsolete room associations. Current immutable preparation receipts intentionally preserve those registry rows.

## Authoritative data and projection

Delivery remains the owner. Forward migration144 adds one explicit revision offering-audience override, represented by a scope parent keyed by `(change_set_id, learning_offering_id)` and allowed-homeroom children. Both carry the existing term/year context and exact owner foreign keys. Parent absence means unchanged legacy semantics; parent presence with zero children means intentionally empty scope. There is no parallel negative room/target mask and no duplicate offering graph. Existing offering/group exclusions remain.

Each explicit room/group removal records the selected snapshot's current effective room set minus the affected rooms. Group removal establishes an override even when that delta is zero, so the projected group exclusion also constrains operational eligibility. Explicit whole-offering removal also records an EMPTY audience parent, besides its offering exclusion. A scoped re-add replaces that parent/children with the exact reviewed current-curriculum audience. Ordinary active Retain may union only explicitly reviewed valid additions. This freezes the approved set: a later M4 homeroom added under a stale broad registry grade/program target cannot re-enter it automatically.

Extend the single `academic_delivery_revision_snapshot` projection. Apply the override consistently to offering targets, offering `homeroomIds`, and group coverage. Preserve a partially retained group's ID, name, description, capacity, teachers and other configuration. Omit a previously room-covered group when restriction removes its entire coverage; preserve originally unscoped/manual groups unless explicitly removed. With an EMPTY offering audience, omit the offering from the selected graph while retaining its scope parent/registry/history. Nonempty central/deferred audience remains valid without groups.

A partially restricted `grade_program` target must not continue implying excluded or future unreviewed rooms. Project surviving audience as explicit homeroom targets with deterministic snapshot identity using the existing target contract, and deduplicate room targets. Unaffected/no-override representation remains exact. Consumers must not compensate with UI-only filtering.

New revisions copy cumulative scope parents AND their allowed-room children, including empty parents, plus relevant explicit group exclusions from the published base revision. Preserve scope intent even when its offering is absent from the snapshot. Legacy bases without recorded intent retain legacy behavior; migration144 does not infer a retroactive repair for old142 removals. Unrelated edits, candidate-date previews, publication and next-draft creation cannot lose explicit scope.

Scope parent/child mutation is permitted only under the owning writable draft revision; published/cancelled scope intent is protected with exact parent/context constraints and immutability guards. Existing legal guarded draft/version cleanup remains supported. Migration144 never rebuilds published/cached graphs as a backfill.

## Operations and HTTP contract

Keep existing whole-offering and group DELETE routes and their `RemoveDeliveryDraftResourceRequest` unchanged. Add:

- `DELETE /api/academic/term-change-sets/{id}/offerings/{offeringId}/homerooms/{homeroomId}`
- operation ID `removeDeliveryDraftOfferingHomeroom`
- request `RemoveDeliveryDraftResourceRequest { changeSetRowVersion: i64, targetDeliveryVersionRowVersion: i64 }`
- response existing `AcademicTermChangeSet`
- typed Delivery provider `remove_draft_offering_homeroom`.

Explicit room removal writes the selected offering audience minus that room and removes it from projected group coverage. Group removal always records its group exclusion and a scope parent. The branch is explicit: for a synchronized activity (`kind=activity`, `schedulingMode=synchronized`), group removal PRESERVES the selected audience, including removal of its last group. Only explicit room removal (or whole-offering removal/reviewed re-add) changes that central participation audience; never apply the generic group-exclusive-room formula to it. For other offerings, audience impact is removed-group rooms minus rooms covered by other surviving selected groups. Zero delta keeps the unchanged selected audience while still activating projected group exclusion. An already group-less deferred/central offering retains its explicit nonempty audience; absence of groups alone never derives an empty audience.


Clear selected-revision pending group commands only for fully omitted groups. Preserve commands/configuration for partially retained groups and unrelated offerings. Retain term/lifecycle, owner management, selected membership and both revision guards. Lock the term, revision/version, offering and affected groups in established stable order. One atomic mutation increments the parent/target revisions once and records its audience/group impact in the existing audit owner. Stale/absent selected room is a conflict; denied writes do not mutate anything. No destructive retry or raw registry coverage update.

## Scoped curriculum preparation and re-add

Add optional `deliveryVersionId` to `PreviewCurriculumOfferingsRequest`. It remains absent only for existing unscoped/term-preparation behavior. Selected-draft preview and apply use the same explicit version context; apply's existing `deliveryVersionId` is retained. A scoped apply cannot accept a hash built without its selected context.

Bind `sourceHash` to the selected version/revision identity and row versions, explicit audience presence/allowed rooms and group/offering exclusions, current curriculum publication sources, reviewed proposals and effective retained groups. Recompute under apply locks before writes; return conflict after any source/draft change. Extra public row-version fields are unnecessary when this quote includes and validates both existing counters.

For an active included offering, Retain preserves valid existing audience/configuration and applies only the explicit reviewed addition. For an offering absent from the selected graph with an explicit scope parent (including empty) or an existing offering exclusion, re-add reconciles its selected audience to the reviewed current-curriculum room set. Absence must not erase that scope-presence distinction. Keep only that reviewed audience; retain valid M6/1 group IDs/settings/teachers instead of creating replacements. Build proposals/conflicts from this authoritative prospective scope, so fully omitted M4 groups do not generate `generated_group_target_mismatch`. Compare retained generated-group coverage against the projected audience: a combined raw group trimmed to M6/1 is retained without rewriting registry coverage. A genuinely conflicting retained manual group or mismatching effective generated coverage remains a conflict. DeferGroups must not restore excluded groups implicitly. Any explicit restoration clears only the reviewed affected exclusion, not all restrictions.

Preserve existing deterministic preparation IDs and Lifecycle term-preparation predictions. Preserve immutable apply receipts and exact-key replay; replay returns its prior result and performs no restoration. A newly reviewed operation uses a new key. Keep catalog/source provenance accurate; never overwrite the old publication evidence.

## Effective operational consumers

Expose a connection-bound typed OPTIONAL Delivery audience constraint over the new canonical scope owner. Dated lookup chooses the published Delivery source effective on or before the supplied operation date; pinned lookup uses the exact supplied timetable source. If that source has no explicit applicable override, return None and preserve existing registry workflow semantics, including standalone offerings before first Delivery publication. If an override exists, return Some with its exact canonical offering/group audience, INCLUDING empty; never fall back to registry coverage for Some(empty) or an omitted scoped offering.

Concrete prospective eligibility owners are Delivery `activities::list_registration_options_on_connection` and `require_eligible_student` (new self-registration), `groups::roster_source` (roster candidates, reached by preview/apply/publication), `roster_tracking::sync_group` (automatic additions from admission/placement events), and `roster_memberships::add_membership` (explicit new dated manual membership). They consume this constraint, not foreign-table reconstructions. Preserve the existing admission/placement adapters; no Admission rewrite is needed merely because those events reach the existing Delivery tracking owner. A future scope takes effect on its published effective date, not while draft or merely when publication occurred. Draft views may show selected scope but cannot apply it globally to operational rosters before that boundary.

`roster_memberships::add_membership` resolves the applicable published constraint at `request.joined_at`, under its existing term/group/roster/manual-policy guards, before inserting any membership. With Some, the offering AND group must exist in that canonical source; empty/omitted scope denies the new intake. Within a retained group, preserve the existing deliberate manual exception: a manager may add a student outside that student's own homeroom under existing policy. Do not invent a blanket manual-add homeroom check. None retains the original behavior. Existing membership date, overlap, student-year, capacity, permission and revision checks remain.

Verified persisted provenance is `roster_source`: `groups::apply_roster` writes `placement` for generated candidates and `manual_add` for explicit Add overrides (`groups.rs:733–764`); `roster_memberships::add_membership` writes `operational_change` (`roster_memberships.rs:194–196`); automatic tracking writes `homeroom_tracking` (`roster_tracking.rs:369`). The attendance owner uses those facts rather than consumer guesses.

Existing roster episodes, explicit manual overrides and score/assessment history are not erased or globally truncated by scope removal. Normal dated enrollment/transfer behavior stays with its owner. In automatic reconciliation, distinguish students denied a NEW join by the scope from actual departures; do not feed the scope filter into a generic missing-candidate branch that destroys preexisting membership history. Current eligibility is a constraint over existing membership facts, not a second roster ledger.

Timetable placement/demand already uses its pinned Delivery graph. Keep explicit draft source adoption and source-mismatch publication guards. Removed groups or changed combined coverage require explicit reconciliation before new timetable publication; existing published placements are never rewritten.

Attendance is the required pinned-source adapter. `attendance::published_spans` and dated block adapters must retain the timetable's Delivery source context. Delivery `attendance_roster::students_on_connection`/membership-span projection currently accepts only group IDs/date and would include registry M4 members of a partially retained M6-only group. Add a source-aware projection: None constraint retains its existing behavior; Some uses that exact source's offering/group coverage and dated membership eligibility. Preserve old pinned-source attendance even after newer Delivery publication; never substitute latest Delivery. First require the offering/group to be retained by Some: no provenance bypasses an omitted offering/group or explicit empty scope. Within a retained group, preserve dated `manual_add` and `operational_change` memberships as deliberate manual exceptions even outside the student's own homeroom. Generated `placement` and `homeroom_tracking` memberships use temporal source-room eligibility. Keep self-registration's existing eligibility meaning; do not silently reclassify it as a manager override. Unknown/legacy historical provenance retains existing manual-fact meaning in retained groups rather than being guessed automatic or rewritten. Keep this policy in Delivery, not a consumer-side current-room filter. No membership or earned-grade rows are deleted by attendance projection.

Do not current-filter historical gradebooks, earned results, criteria or corrections. Concrete raw assessment readers include Exam workspace `EXAM_SOURCE_PREVIEW_SQL`/`insert_exam_source_in_tx` and paper receipt sources, plus Results `term_preview`/`activity_preview`; these use persisted assessment/membership/result obligations. They are not automatically redefined as new Delivery admissions by this room-association fix. Keep established obligations/history intact. Required readiness scope in this change is projected Delivery readiness, Timetable source reconciliation, and the concrete new-intake/roster eligibility boundaries above, not a blanket assessment/history cutover.

## UI scope

In the room workspace, both normal and extra offering rows expose only the room-scoped action `นำออกจากห้องนี้`. Whole-offering removal remains in the subject/activity tab and course detail; do not duplicate both destructive actions on each room row. Detail group removal still removes the selected group across its selected covered rooms and confirms those rooms explicitly.

Display combined-group coverage from the selected snapshot. Keep the raw registry editor entity separate; do not replace its coverage with the projected subset and accidentally save that subset globally. When projected and registry coverage differ, explain the scope clearly and gate raw coverage editing unless a separately explicit supported operation is available. Settings/teachers remain usable under their existing lifecycle and permission guards. Room removal does not delete manual memberships/grades or automatically publish a roster/timetable.
