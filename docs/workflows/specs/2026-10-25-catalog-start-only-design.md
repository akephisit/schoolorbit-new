# Central catalogs with start-only revisions

Approved scope: central subject and activity identities and revisions have no academic-year binding or expiry date. Published revisions become eligible on their civil `effective_from` date. Current metadata selects the greatest eligible start date, then greatest revision number; upcoming metadata selects the earliest future start date, then greatest revision. Drafts never become current. Catalog expiry states and inputs disappear from Rust DTOs, generated contracts and School UI.

School Academic Core owns catalog persistence and selection. Curriculum continues to admit the current subject revision on Bangkok's current civil date. Delivery retains explicit published version pins when their start date is no later than the selected term start; it does not force old pins to current. Curriculum history, offering snapshots, results and teacher/group membership lifecycles retain their identities and facts. Derived delivery/timetable ends and actual year/term dates are outside this change.

Subject semantic duplicate checks retain their current scope and stable-subject lock, with expiry removed from applicability and equality. Activities use their existing stable-activity locks and revision-number uniqueness; interval overlap rejection is retired, and no new activity content-deduplication policy is introduced.

Unapplied forward migration 138 locks the catalog and actual reference graph, preserves the exact old year UUID and nullable expiry under `migration_provenance.historicalCatalogContext`, and retains every prior provenance object. Existing versions whose former expiry has passed become eligible under the new policy intentionally. The migration rejects unsupported provenance, reserved-key collisions, disabled expected guards and unknown dependencies. It retires the activity interval trigger/function before metadata backfill, temporarily disables only the two published-version guards and inherited subject timestamp trigger inside its atomic transaction, restores those three guards, removes exact obsolete constraints/indexes/columns with RESTRICT, and reconciles all remaining facts and references before an aggregate audit. Published rows remain immutable after cutover. The DTO `migrated` meaning ignores only the new historical-context namespace.

Deploy matched backend/contracts/frontend behind universal maintenance and canonical all-tenant migration/readiness gates. Earlier binaries cannot run against schema 138. Keep maintenance on failure and repair forward; never rewrite historical records or migration history to force acceptance. No load tests, tenant fixture cleanup, year retargeting or manual production SQL are authorized.
